# Boosts — the ramp-up system

Temporary, high-salience interests I'm into **right now**. Separate from the stable
baseline in [[interests.md]] on purpose: boosts are *meant to expire*. A boosted topic
scores as if it were a very-high-weight interest (baseline 5 + boost) and gets first
priority in the discovery phase. Level: +1 nudge, +3 strong, +5 flood my feed.

**Controls (just tell the sweep):** "ramp up X" · "dial down X" · "make X passive" (moves it
into interests.md) · "drop X".
